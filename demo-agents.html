Agent Architecture · Model Fleet
4 models · routed by task profile
A360 routes each agent to the model that best fits its job profile. Heavy clinical reasoning runs on Sonnet; fast gates and validators run on Haiku to keep latency and cost low; sales/marketing generation runs on GPT-4o where its tone is strongest; coaching uses Gemini 2.5 Flash for retrieval-grounded responses. We deliberately keep the fleet small (4 models) so prompt-eval, cost monitoring, and failover stay tractable.
Workhorse · clinical
Claude Sonnet 4.5
Heavy reasoning, structured extraction, clinical narrative generation, treatment planning
Fast · cheap · gates
Claude Haiku 4.5
Routing gates, validators, simple classifiers, preprocessing — sub-second responses at low cost
Sales · marketing · copy
GPT-4o
Cross-sell guidance, opportunity narratives, Reach email + SMS — tone strength for direct-response copy
RAG · coaching · long context

Why 4 models, not 7? Each additional model in the fleet doubles eval-suite cost and adds a new prompt-tuning surface. We pick the smallest fleet that covers the four distinct task profiles (heavy reasoning · fast gates · sales copy · RAG/coaching). Adding a fifth requires a real new capability the existing four can't deliver — not a marginal quality bump.
