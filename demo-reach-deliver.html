Delivery Options

Two ways to ship a Reach campaign. Generate Complete Campaign writes every email and SMS for staff review before send. Push to CRM sends the structured signals to GHL templates, letting the practice's existing automation system handle the messaging. Most practices use both — full content for high-intent patients, CRM push for the long tail.
Option 1
Generate Complete Campaign
Reach writes everything · staff approves · GHL delivers

Reach generates fully-written, ready-to-send content. The practice gets a single review surface where they approve or edit before anything goes out. Best for high-intent patients where personalization depth matters most.

  • ✓5 Email messages — subject lines, A/B variants, full body, CTA, education links
  • ✓3 SMS messages — standard, character-conscious, opt-out compliant
  • ✓Conversational SMS script — full OrchardLink RCS branching tree with quick-reply CTAs
  • ✓Send schedule — exact dates, time-of-day optimized per patient timezone
  • ✓A/B subject line variants — 2-3 alternates per email for testing
  • ✓Education content links — pulled from practice library
  • ✓Compliance checks — no medical claims, opt-out present, HIPAA-safe
Staff Review
Required before sending · estimated 2 minutes per campaign for approve-or-edit

Option 2
Push Intelligence to CRM
Reach sets fields · GHL templates handle messaging

Reach pushes structured contact-field data to GoHighLevel. The practice's existing templates and workflows fire on those fields — the practice keeps using the system they know, but every message is now consultation-aware. Best for the long tail and for practices that prefer to own their messaging.
