The A360 platform, made visible.

A walkthrough of what A360 captures from a real medical aesthetics consultation, what it produces downstream, and how the pieces of the platform fit together. Each section is its own page — open them individually or use the top nav.

⚠️

This is a demo environment, not the actual product UI.

These pages exist to visualize what A360 does — what data it captures, how it reasons, what it produces. The shipping product will have far more focus on optimized UX, accessibility, and the hundred small details that make a clinical tool feel right. Treat the layouts here as illustrative, not finished. The data behind them is real wherever possible — real consultation extractions, real eval reports, real practice catalog data — and clearly labeled as mocked when not.

What this is

A360 turns medical aesthetics consultations into structured intelligence. A live consultation gets captured, extracted, evaluated, and pushed downstream into care plans, marketing campaigns, and provider coaching.

This site walks through that pipeline one component at a time. Each component below opens its own page. Click any small i icon on a card inside those pages to see exactly which agent powered it, what data it consumed, and where the data came from.

Some sections are wired to live backend services (Live Extraction, Accuracy Lab). Others render curated examples drawn from real consultations (Extraction, Agents, TCP Builder, Intelligence). The post-consultation surfaces (Reach) use mocked content while the underlying agents are still in design — every personalization phrase is traced back to a specific extraction signal so the underlying logic is auditable even when the delivery is staged.
