Why this campaign type · the 9 the agent picks from decision tree
The agent reads the consultation signals and selects from 9 campaign archetypes. Sarah's unresolved price objection + medium-high intent + event urgency → VALUE_REINFORCEMENT. Different signals → different campaigns. No two patients get the same plan.
BOOKING_FACILITATION
Committed patient · remove friction → booking. 3-4 emails, aggressive cadence.
HIGH_INTEREST_NURTURE
Interested patient · build confidence. 4-5 emails, medium cadence, mix of edu + booking.
EVENT_DRIVEN
Anchored to a date · timed backward from the event. 4-5 emails, weekly cadence.
SELECTED
VALUE_REINFORCEMENT
Unresolved price objection · reframe cost as investment, financing math. 4-5 emails, moderate cadence.
REASSURANCE_EDUCATION
Fear / anxiety objection · safety + natural results. 4-5 emails, calm tone.
STANDARD_FOLLOWUP
Considering · balanced education + engagement. 4 emails, standard cadence.
GENTLE_ENGAGEMENT
Uncertain / low-intent · stay top-of-mind. 3 emails, slow cadence, pure education.
AFTERCARE_REBOOKING
Treatment performed · aftercare + maintenance rebooking. 4 emails timed to treatment duration.
CROSS_SELL_EXPANSION
Future interests engaged · introduce complementary treatments. Delayed-start, runs alongside primary.
Stacking rule: a patient may qualify for multiple types — never run more than 2 simultaneous campaigns. Cross-sell always eligible as secondary, delayed-start. Total emails capped at 10 per 30-day window. Patient response (open, click, reply) pauses automation and flags for human follow-up.
