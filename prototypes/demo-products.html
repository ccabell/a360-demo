Fractional radiofrequency microneedling device for skin rejuvenation, tissue remodeling, and collagen stimulation
Device FDA Cleared (510(k)) InMode
Treatment Time

12-18 months; results build with each treatment
Sessions
1-3 sessions, 4-6 weeks apart
Downtime

Mechanism
Combines fractional microneedling with bipolar RF energy. Gold-coated needles penetrate to adjustable depths (0.5-4mm) delivering RF heat that triggers deep collagen remodeling.

1-2mm superficial (fine lines, texture, pores)

Common Objections & Responses 4
"It's expensive — why not just use a dermaroller at home?"
At-home dermarollers only penetrate the surface and don't deliver any RF energy. Morpheus8 reaches depths of up to 4mm AND delivers radiofrequency heat that triggers deep collagen remodeling — something no home device can do.
"I can't afford to take days off for recovery"
Most patients have redness similar to a sunburn for 2-3 days. Schedule Thursday or Friday, back to normal by Monday. Mineral makeup can be applied after 24-48 hours.
"Microneedling with needles — doesn't that hurt?"
We apply strong topical numbing cream 30-45 minutes before. Most patients feel only pressure and warmth. On a comfort scale, most rate it 3-5 out of 10.
"I've seen scary TikTok videos — is it really safe?"
Social media shows the most dramatic moments, not the full story. In experienced hands, Morpheus8 is very safe with millions of treatments worldwide. The key is having a trained provider who understands proper settings for your skin type.
