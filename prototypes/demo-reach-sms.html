Why Conversational SMS Changes Everything

Standard SMS · push-only

Practice → Patient. The patient reads the message or doesn't. If they have a question, they have to call the office during business hours, or they don't bother and the lead cools. Three messages over 21 days, then silence.

Conversational RCS · two-way

Practice ↔ Patient. The patient engages on their own time. AI has full consultation context — knows the wedding, the price objection, the package, the fear of looking "frozen." Answers in seconds, books appointments, escalates to staff only when needed.
